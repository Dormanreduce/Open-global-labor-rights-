# Open-global-labor-rights-
An open data project documenting global labor exploitation, human rights crises, and ethical supply chains.
## About the Project
This open knowledge project is initiated by a Sociology and Anthropology student. Our goal is to collect, analyze, and open-source data regarding modern labor exploitation, unethical manufacturing, and human rights crises in conflict zones (such as Congo and Sudan) and globally.

## Objectives
- To translate complex sociological and human rights reports into accessible open data.
- To provide resources for ethical creators, journalists, and human rights advocates.
- To raise awareness about the true cost of global supply chains.

## Current Status
This project is currently in its early data-gathering phase. We are using AI tools to process qualitative research and will soon publish our first open-access datasets.
